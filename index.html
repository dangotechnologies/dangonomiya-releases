What it does

One app for the two games, and a bot that keeps an eye on them while you're away.

Scans from the screen

Characters, weapons, artifacts and materials, read with OCR while it goes through the menus for you. Nothing is injected into the game.

Wishes and Convenes

Your full history with pity per banner, soft pity marks, every 5★ with the 50/50 you won or lost, and a heatmap of the days you pulled.

A dashboard you arrange

Dailies, Welkin, Battle Pass, resin, Pioneer Podcast and Lunite, each counted from the right reset. Drag the tiles, hide what you don't need.

A Discord bot

Daily and weekly reports, missions you set yourself, and alerts when resin reaches your threshold or expeditions come back.

Check-in and codes

With your HoYoLAB account connected, the app does the daily check-in and redeems new promo codes for you.

Keeps itself up to date

New versions download in the background and install the next time you restart the app.

How it works

A few minutes the first time, then it mostly runs on its own.

Install and open the game

Dangonomiya finds Genshin Impact or Wuthering Waves on your PC and knows when the game is running.

Scan what you want

Pick characters, inventory or the daily menus and let it click through them. An overlay shows what it reads.

Link the bot, if you like

Use /setup in Discord and the app links your account to a server or to your DMs.

Questions

The short answers.

Is it safe for my account?

The scanner only looks at the screen and uses the mouse and keyboard like you would. It doesn't read the game's memory, change its files or inject anything.

Where do my HoYoLAB cookies go?

They stay on your PC, encrypted for your Windows user. The Discord bot never keeps them; the app does the check-in and code redeeming itself and only reports the results.

Do I need the Discord bot?

No. Scans, wishes and the dashboard work on their own. The bot adds reports, missions and alerts.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a notice the first time. Choose "More info" and then "Run anyway".
